Figure 2c -- |E|^2 mode map of the tuned L3 cavity, junction site marked (out/m2/l3_3d_final_s020_rdef115_res18_Efield.npz; photonics.json:modes, geometry).

Mid-slab (z-index=23 of the res=18 grid) steady-state |E|^2 field intensity, normalized to its own maximum and shown on a log color scale to expose both the strongly-confined cavity mode and its weaker in-plane leakage into the surrounding lattice (hole outline overlaid in blue, contoured at the air/slab eps midpoint, eps_core=11.68). x and y are in units of the lattice constant a (MEEP normalized units, scale-invariant): the field pattern shown is unchanged whether a is set to the value used inside this run (a=638.0132 um, the AT-0/AT-1 bulk-midgap lattice constant used for this isolated-cavity MEEP run) or rescaled to the final device value a=586.7353 um (photonics.json:geometry.a_um_precise, the cavity-line-fixed lattice constant). The junction site (cyan cross) sits at the cavity centre (x=y=0), coincident with the field maximum.

Mode volume: V_mode = 0.3307 a^3 = 6.679279e+07 um^3 at the device a (V_mode convention: integral(eps|E|^2 dV) / max(eps|E|^2); photonics.json:modes.V_mode_convention). Run parameters: drive fcen=0.290 (reduced frequency), isolated 3D L3 cavity (s=0.20, rdef=1.15), from the AT-2 lossy res18 winner (out/m2/l3_3d_final_s020_rdef115_res18.json).
