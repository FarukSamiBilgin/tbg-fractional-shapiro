Figure 2a -- TE-like photonic-crystal-slab band structure at the device lattice constant (out/m2/fig2a_bands.json; scripts/m2_fig2a_bands.py; photonics.json:geometry, modes).

3D MPB band structure of the BULK triangular air-hole lattice in the HR-Si slab at the locked device geometry: r/a = 0.3, slab thickness t/a = 0.5, eps_core = 11.68, air cladding, supercell height 4.0 a in z, solved with run_zeven (TE-like: E in-plane, even about the mid-slab z = 0 mirror plane) at in-plane resolution 20 px/a (80 px across the z supercell), 8 bands on 52 k-points along Gamma-M-K-Gamma (16 interpolated points per segment). Left axis is the MPB reduced frequency nu = a/lambda (units c/a); the right axis is the same quantity in GHz at the DEVICE lattice constant a = 586.735322 um (photonics.json:geometry.a_um_precise, the cavity-line-fixed a; 510.9501 GHz per unit nu). Note this is a different a from the AT-0 bulk-midgap-centred a = 638.0132 um used in earlier M2 band summaries -- the reduced-frequency band table is scale free, so only the GHz axis moves.

Grey shading is the air light cone f_LL(k) = |k_cart|/(2 pi); modes above it are leaky and are drawn faded. Dark blue are the two lowest guided TE-like bands (band 1 = dielectric band, band 2 = air band) restricted to k-points where they lie below the light line; medium blue are the higher guided bands 3-8.

Gap edges (max of band 1 over guided k / min of band 2 over guided k): lower 0.269040 a/lambda at K, upper 0.348133 a/lambda at M, i.e. [137.4660, 177.8786] GHz at the device a, gap width 0.079093 a/lambda = 40.413 GHz. The 145.000 GHz cavity line (nu_0 = 0.283785, photonics.json:modes.f0_GHz) sits inside the gap with margins +7.53 GHz above the lower edge and +32.88 GHz below the upper edge.

Consistency (this is a re-measurement of an already-locked number, not a new one): photonics.json:modes.cavity_line_gap_GHz = [137.5, 177.9] GHz. Measured here: [137.4660, 177.8786] GHz, deviation (-0.0340, -0.0214) GHz = (-0.0247%, -0.0120%), inside the 0.1% MPB internal-convergence tolerance the project uses; the residual is at the level of the 0.1 GHz rounding with which photonics.json quotes those edges. The gap edges also reproduce the AT-0 step-2 run (out/m2/at0_slab3d.json, 10 interpolated points per segment) to 2.8e-09 in a/lambda -- both k-paths contain the K and M corners exactly, where the edges sit, so this is solver repeatability rather than an independent check.

Convergence: repeating the two edge k-points at resolution 30 (x1.5) gives lower edge 0.268781 and upper edge 0.348031 a/lambda, i.e. edge drifts of 0.0963% (lower) and 0.0292% (upper) and a midgap drift of 0.0584% (-0.0921 GHz). Both edges move down slightly with resolution; the lower edge carries the larger residual grid bias (0.0963%, i.e. 0.132 GHz, right at the 0.1% convergence tolerance), and the midgap is converged to 0.0584%. The res-20 numbers are the ones carried by photonics.json and are quoted here unchanged; this residual is the dominant uncertainty on the plotted edges, larger than the 0.0340/0.0214 GHz agreement with photonics.json.

Run: MEEP/MPB 1.34.0, Python 3.13.14, serial, inside the m2env WSL distro (conda env mp). Deterministic -- no RNG. Total solver time 186.4 s (139.1 s for the 52-point res-20 path, 47.3 s for the 2 res-30 edge k-points). Checkpoint/resume state: out/m2/fig2a_bands_ck.json.
