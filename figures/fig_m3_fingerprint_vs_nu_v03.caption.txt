Figure 3b (paper version, contract v0.3) -- Fractional Shapiro-step fingerprint vs filling nu.

(a) Deterministic half-integer (v=Omega/2) step width w_1/2(nu) at its i_ac optimum (measured Beenakker tau=0.8 CPR, material.json v0.2 dome), in units of I_c(nu) (left axis, unchanged under v0.3: IcRn = 60 uV is invariant, so the entire reduced-unit problem -- Omega(nu), i_ac_opt(nu), CPR shape, w_1/2(nu) -- is identical to the v0.2 archive) and in absolute nA at v0.3 currents (right axis, x10 vs the v0.2 archive figure: dome-center step 6.0 nA -> 60.0 nA). Filled circles are the 5 independently simulated fillings (nu = -2.4 .. -2.8, scripts/run_fingerprint_nu.py, deterministic RK4, spp=60, no RNG); open circles are their dome-symmetric mirror copies about nu_opt = -2.4 (material.json). Each point is annotated with its optimal drive amplitude i_ac_opt(nu).

(b) Two-branch noisy survival overlay at each of the 5 simulated fillings' i_ac_opt(nu): ensemble-mean noisy half-step width divided by the deterministic width, W_1/2/w_1/2^det(nu) (stochastic-Heun Langevin, spp=100, n_real=20 realizations per point, out/nu_survival.json). Both branches use the SAME Omega(nu) and i_ac_opt(nu) (the intrinsic, Gamma-independent dome physics) and differ ONLY in the thermal parameter Gamma, so their shared shape along the nu axis isolates the intrinsic Omega(nu) suppression while the vertical gap between them at fixed nu (dotted connectors) isolates the thermal suppression:
  - v0.2 narrow-junction archive branch: Gamma_v0.2(nu) (0.140 at nu=-2.4 rising to 0.389 at nu=-2.8), seeds = 20260725100 + round(100*|nu|) + 0 -> nu=-2.8: seed 20260725380, nu=-2.7: seed 20260725370, nu=-2.6: seed 20260725360, nu=-2.5: seed 20260725350, nu=-2.4: seed 20260725340.
  - v0.3 production branch: Gamma_v0.3(nu) = Gamma_v0.2(nu)/10 (0.014 at nu=-2.4 rising to 0.039 at nu=-2.8), seeds = 20260725100 + round(100*|nu|) + 1 -> nu=-2.8: seed 20260725381, nu=-2.7: seed 20260725371, nu=-2.6: seed 20260725361, nu=-2.5: seed 20260725351, nu=-2.4: seed 20260725341.
Error bars are +/-1 standard deviation of the per-realization step width across the n_real=20 realizations, normalized by w_1/2^det(nu).

Caveat (Omega=13.9 edge point, nu=-2.8): Omega(nu) = f_drive / f_c(nu) is computed holding the normal-state resistance R_n FIXED at its dome-center (nu=-2.4) contract value across the whole nu-scan, while only I_c(nu) varies with filling per the measured dome (material.json); a real device's R_n would generically also vary with filling/junction width away from the dome center, which this Rn(nu)-fixed treatment does not capture. The nu=-2.8 point, with the largest Omega excursion in the scan (Omega=13.9 vs 5.0 at the dome center), is the most exposed to this approximation and should be read with that caveat; its large apparent w_1/2 (0.278 Ic, 0.2778 reduced) and its thermal-branch gap (v0.2 survival 0.25 vs v0.3 survival 0.50) are measured self-consistently within this convention but are the least directly transferable to a fixed-geometry device sweep.

Cross-check: the v0.3 branch at nu=-2.4 (Omega=5, i_ac=3.5, Gamma=0.014) independently reproduces the dedicated Gamma-scan production point (out/survival_curve.json, task A1) within realization-to-realization noise: nu_survival.json survival_fraction=0.625 vs survival_curve.json production_v0p3 survival=0.750 (different seeds, same Omega/i_ac/Gamma regime).
